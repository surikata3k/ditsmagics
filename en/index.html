What is Dits Màgics?

Dits Màgics (“magic fingers” in Catalan) is a free app that teaches children to touch type in English. Each level introduces new keys: first the home row, then the top and bottom rows, and step by step words, capital letters, apostrophes, punctuation, numbers and sentences. It is also available in Catalan and Spanish.

The on-screen keyboard highlights the next key in green and shows which finger to use. Depending on your accuracy you earn up to three stars, and one star unlocks the next level. The keyboard shown is the Spanish layout. No sign-up needed: your progress is saved in your browser.
